# Research Methodology

This document outlines the qualitative and quantitative methodology used to validate the vague-memory retrieval problem.

## 1. Discovery Engine (Quantitative Context)
Before conducting interviews, we use an AI-powered pipeline to ingest unstructured complaints from Reddit, app store reviews, and forums. 
The AI clusters these into "Failure Modes" and "Memory Cues" to form a foundational understanding of the landscape.

## 2. User Interviews (Qualitative Deep Dive)
**Sample Size:** 5-6 Target Users
**Criteria:** Users who actively manage a photo library of 5,000+ images and frequently search for past memories.

## 3. Evidence Synthesis
All findings are logged as `OBSERVATION`s. When multiple observations converge, they form an `OPPORTUNITY`. We do not rely on AI to auto-select the winning opportunity; we manually synthesize the findings to ensure human judgment.
