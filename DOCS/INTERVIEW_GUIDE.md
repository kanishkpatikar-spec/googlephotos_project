# User Interview Protocol

## Setup (5 mins)
"Thank you for joining. Today we are talking about how you find old photos in your library. There are no right or wrong answers."

## Context Gathering (10 mins)
1. Tell me about your photo library. (Which app? How many photos?)
2. Do you organize your photos manually, or rely on search?

## The Core Task (20 mins)
"I want you to think of a specific photo you know you took, but you haven't looked at in over a year."
1. What do you remember about it right now? (Capture Memory Cues)
2. Try to find it using your current app. (Observe process)
3. Did you find it? What was the breaking point? (Capture Failure Mode)

## Wrap-up (5 mins)
"If you could have a magic wand to help you find that photo, what would it do?"
